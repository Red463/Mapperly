## Explicit limits

- Timbermaw (native WMO 111596, placement 372018): all three branch pairs are bundled. Native short stair risers may face either direction and occur outside steps_* groups. Endpoints join the owning-zone components containing Bloodvenom Post/Emerald Sanctuary and Everlook; the numerically largest rectangle components belong to surrounding zones. Reputation may affect safe passage.
- Winterspring–Hyjal cave (WMO 7960309, placement 70062766): the apparent Hyjal-side floor is at least 196 yards below terrain and has no supported outdoor Hyjal landing. It is not accepted as a through-exit.
- Ironforge main entrance and Undercity static sewer entrance are bundled as native-floor corridors to permitted interior anchors. This certifies those entrance passages, not every corridor, wall or vertical layer throughout either city. Undercity elevators are separate transport work.
- The Great Lift remains a transport connection, not a walking edge. Separate boats, zeppelins, trams and discovered flights belong to the next navigation milestone.
- Gilneas ownership chunks are present in the snapshot but have no supported local map assignment in this dataset; no connection is fabricated through them.
- Native WMO corridors retain their winding centerlines and require a dry outdoor landing or a separately proven interior floor anchor. Stair/floor/headroom checks are sampled native geometry, not a swept character collision certificate. Dynamic doors, faction restrictions and reputation requirements need live acceptance.
- Routes use bounded local searches. A connected zone graph does not promise every point is reachable or that every far journey fits the work budget.
