# Journey navigation design

Build a standalone journey engine behind the existing coordinate, marker and slash-command interfaces. The first milestone plans local walking paths from bundled Forever terrain masks; later milestones connect validated local paths through a directed network of zone crossings and transport edges.

A plan is a sequence of typed legs with geometry, approximate time/distance, source/coverage and availability information. The HUD follows the current leg instead of always pointing directly at the destination. Map lines and the preview window render the same plan. Pause/resume/skip/replan operate on this shared state.

Routing runs as cancellable coroutine jobs with frame work budgets. A successful request atomically installs its destination and history; failures preserve the old active destination. Terrain failures are reported instead of silently creating straight-line walking routes. Direct-bearing navigation remains an explicit user choice.

Use separate modules: Terrain (validated local geometry), PlanningJob (cooperative work), Journey (plan and follower), JourneyView (map lines/preview), NavigationSettings (preferences), TravelNetwork (directed transports/zone crossings), QuestSearch (quest-to-destination adapter), Roads (verified road metadata), JourneyReport (export). Reuse owned Gatherly terrain code/data with provenance. Do not copy Shortest Path Forever code.

The Navigation settings tab will contain route mode, guidance visibility, recalculation and road/swimming/transport preferences as their backing features become available. Do not show functional-looking toggles for unsupported data.

Quest scope begins with active quests. A later bundled catalogue must identify source, objective/turn-in ambiguity and coverage. Transport times are approximate without observed schedules; unknown flights are excluded until discovered or explicitly confirmed.

Player movement may leave the terrain mask without stopping an installed journey. Restricted player cells preserve the current route/waypoint and permit manual resume, skip and normal arrival advancement. This does not make restricted cells valid planning endpoints or rewrite any planned segment. Missing/protected positions and unsupported zones still pause directional guidance. Verified multi-zone plans hand off between local legs at their validated crossings.

Smart routing is a default-on preference stored through the existing terrain/direct mode for compatibility. Changes apply immediately to an active or pending destination. Off cancels terrain work and activates the already validated destination directly; on starts a transactional walking request. Stale previews close on a mode change. Minimap and world-map visibility are independent; direct plans use live player world coordinates and remain available without terrain coverage.

Cross-zone walking bundles native area/road-derived short connectors and map extents. Multi-zone plans retain flat mixed-map points for display and local leg first/last indices for following. Direct adjacent connections are preferred; a bounded network search handles other supported paths. RoutePosition projects overlapping native assignments into the active leg until its crossing is reached. Leg handoff retains the entry waypoint so delayed zone changes never skip connector geometry. Recovery operates only within the current leg and retains its retry cooldown across transitions.

Scheduled transports are directed edges between dry land approaches with separate native vessel stops and sailing pieces. A typed transport leg owns boarding/travel/disembarking instructions, retains state during unavailable positions, and advances only at the destination approach. Piers and tower access remain manual unless their floors are separately certified. Native ground height prevents treating an elevated deck as ground arrival; protected-height cases can confirm Continue on foot at the destination. Ordinary bend skipping and detour recovery do not skip or replan transit legs.

Transport availability combines native endpoint evidence, explicit conservative faction policy and saved boat/zeppelin/airship choices. Same-vessel consecutive stops merge into one instruction to stay aboard. Mixed plans show walking distance separately from approximate total time; unknown durations and waiting use disclosed planning assumptions. Blue sailing geometry never joins across native teleports or manual handoffs. The bounded search prefers a feasible short zone itinerary and does not promise a global travel-time optimum.

Routing preference changes while aboard apply after reaching land. They cancel stale work and retain the latest pending or already-deferred destination; repeated changes and switching to direct mode cannot resurrect an older destination. An accepted manual replacement, cancellation or stop clears deferred work. These rules extend the original transactional destination/history contract.

Flight journeys use only source-master offerings observed for the current validated character GUID. Native geometry supplies no discovery. Preserve each complete directed itinerary and whole fare; never infer reverse flights, intermediate discoveries or split fares. Incomplete hop geometry is omitted. Prices are explicitly historical, estimated or unknown; readable money limits cumulative known fares. Faction/class/build changes invalidate incompatible offers. Normal flight selection remains manual.

Flight departure is confirmed by positive UnitOnTaxi evidence. It supersedes an unfinished nearby approach; the walking arrow stays hidden while airborne. Missing/secret state and world-loading intervals retain flight progress and cannot count as on-foot evidence. A valid near-destination landing resumes the next ground leg; platform/building access remains manual. Early landing requests one transactional replan after stable on-foot evidence, retaining the journey on failure. Changes during flight, including routing mode and replacement destinations, wait for landing. Live source-master boarding snapshots can bypass only a zero-length already-at-NPC approach, never grant terrain walking permissions. See Data/Flights-SOURCES.md for coverage and assumptions.

Manual local transports have independently pinned access data and method preferences. Great Lift legs bind verified dry ground to nearby static landing evidence; no moving-platform identity or path is inferred. A readable intermediate height can recognise a ride whose departure sample was missed. Destination proximity alone cannot complete a same-XY lift: compatible height or explicit destination confirmation is required. A verified arrival floor does not require prior departure evidence. Nearby arrival-floor guidance points towards the dry landing marker. Loading retains the leg. Trams and other lifts remain unavailable until their station/floor/access evidence supports a useful connection; disabled controls explain the missing coverage.

Forever may expose constant zero altitude. For lifts whose two landing heights are both far from zero, that value is unavailable evidence and cannot imply movement. Independently audited clear-ground exit disks can establish destination access without altitude or departure samples: they exclude the projected static tower collision, pass native dry/slope evidence and runtime ground/region checks, and connect to the existing handoff. A readable height must match the exit's native range; contradictory live coordinates or instance reject arrival rather than falling back. This is a bounded access exemption, not permission to complete on arbitrary nearby ground or in a shaft.
