# Recorded connections removed

As of1.2.0-rc.4, Mapperly no longer asks players to record, capture or verify tram/lift journeys. The recorder modules and settings control are removed; saved personal recordings are not used for routing.

Known connections are bundled. Settings > Navigation > Travel methods provides independent Trams and Lifts choices. Both Deeprun directions are present automatically; the Great Lift retains its native landing handling. Actual walking, boarding and riding remain player actions, as with boats.

See [native tram sources](../Data/Trams-SOURCES.md) and the [current test guide](testing-1.2.0-rc5.md). Tram access uses normal walking paths or a labeled city portal bearing when indoor paths are unavailable. Additional capital lift floor/access data is not yet bundled.
