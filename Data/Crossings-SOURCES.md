# Outdoor walking crossings

Generated by `tools/build_crossings.py` from the read-only Forever build 70959 snapshot and the owned terrain/road datasets. Inputs are root ADT MCNK area IDs (header offset 52), AreaTable parent-area ancestry and UiMapAssignment zone associations. Native map extents come from `tools/roads/zones.json`. No rectangular-overlap adjacency is invented.

At a native area boundary, search up to 62.5 yards along the boundary normal for a selected road approach. Both sides are preferred, but one road approach may connect to the immediately adjoining terrain cell when a bridge or trail has no road texture. The entire connector must be permitted in **both** local terrain masks, with endpoints inside both map extents and total length at most 120 yards. Nearby duplicates are clustered within 100 yards. The original 49 road-boundary crossings are retained.

For pairs without a road-boundary crossing, independently test adjacent **dry** native boundary samples against both terrain masks. Choose at most three separated crossings per pair, preferring the main connected ground region and proximity to roads. This fills 28 formerly missing pairs with 61 dry-ground links, without requiring a road texture or granting permission across unknown terrain. Independently audited native WMO floors supply additional tunnel links; see [Structures-SOURCES.md](Structures-SOURCES.md). Current counts and every rejected boundary pair are generated in [walking-coverage.md](../docs/walking-coverage.md).

Ordinary boundary links are terrain-estimated connections, not verified collision geometry. Existing terrain masks permit swimming; an ordinary connector can include water. Native bridge floors, tunnels, caves and buildings can differ from root terrain. Ironforge and Undercity entrances therefore use independently proven native-floor corridors, not ordinary root-terrain boundary extraction; this does not certify all indoor city routes. The Barrens–Thousand Needles Great Lift transition is deferred to transport support. Zephras Isle map 2665 is canonicalized to 2521 because the supplied native bounds/terrain instance are identical.

Each local leg is planned and validated using Terrain.Path with the saved road preference. Connectors are revalidated at runtime. Adjacent-zone routing first tries direct verified connections; otherwise a bounded network search uses local walking geometry. It does not claim a globally shortest travel time. Jobs share existing coroutine budgeting and cancellation, with at most 128 local searches, about two million expanded cells, and 512 network nodes per request.

The full mixed-map geometry is used by map/minimap lines and the preview. The follower finishes the current local leg, aims across its connector, waits for the client-reported next map, then follows the next leg. Overlapping map assignments are transformed into the current leg's coordinates to prevent premature jumps. Unexpected/unrepresented terrain fails clearly rather than inserting a straight-line walking route.

Rebuild structures, ground corrections and walking regions first, then run `python tools/build_crossings.py` and `python tools/report_walking_coverage.py`. Artifacts and source inventory remain under `../output/zone-crossings-70959`; the raw client snapshot stays read-only. `tests/cross_zone.py` validates every bundled connector; `tests/walking_coverage.py` plans a complete native Duskwood-to-Dun Morogh journey. Live game acceptance of crossings remains necessary.
