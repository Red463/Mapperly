# Precomputed walking connections

Generated by `tools/build_walking_paths.py` using the bundled Lua 5.1 `Terrain.Path` implementation and the same terrain, ground corrections and road masks used in game. No external routing service, invented roads or addon dependency is used.

Anchors come from verified zone crossing endpoints, available native flight-master handoffs, scheduled transport/lift ground endpoints and the ground ends of proven harbour access corridors. These are exact lookup accelerators, not new graph edges. The existing planner still validates transport availability, faction, offers, fares and floor corridors.

Generation attempts up to 32 fixed pairs per zone, favoring nearby anchor neighbors, in road-off mode and all five road strengths. Successful results only are shipped; disconnected ground regions, failed bounded searches and pairs outside the cap use normal runtime pathfinding. Areas without road data share their effective road-off mode.

Runtime matches a revisioned content signature of resolved terrain, ground and road rows, dimensions and road factor. Every baked polyline is terrain-validated and its endpoints and measured length checked before use. Source SHA256 fingerprints and the output digest are recorded in `tools/data/walking-paths-manifest.json`. Rebuild the dataset when the terrain algorithm or inputs change; a test rejects stale builder inputs. The lightweight runtime signature is a stale-data check, not a cryptographic security boundary.

These paths inherit the source coverage and limitations documented in Terrain-SOURCES.md, Roads-SOURCES.md, RoadsSupplement-SOURCES.md and Crossings-SOURCES.md. They do not establish building access, live collision guarantees or character-specific travel unlocks.

The swimming-policy update revalidated all 3,367 original polylines by checking every allowed-swimming signature and segment. Dry-mode signatures include the native liquid grid and use their own live path cache. See Water-SOURCES.md for native liquid coverage and bridge handling.

For the Dun Algaz tunnels, `python tools/build_walking_paths.py --refresh-maps 1432 1437` runs fresh searches in Loch Modan and the Wetlands, then rechecks every retained signature and segment in all other maps. The same pair cap and search budget apply as in the full build. All three walks between successive tunnels are included for each of the six road settings. The manifest records fresh searches by zone and the number of retained paths separately.
