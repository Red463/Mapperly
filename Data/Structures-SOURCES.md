# Native structure walking corridors

### Dun Algaz

Four placements of the medium dwarven tunnel (WMO 110707) connect Loch Modan to the Wetlands. In south-to-north order their placement IDs are 73025, 126354, 68441769 and 68443158. The first changes map inside the tunnel. The remaining three connect separate outdoor areas on the Wetlands map.

`tools/build_dun_algaz.py` re-extracts each placement from the build 70959 snapshot. Each 190-yard passage has 1,897 centreline positions, including both dry landings. Transformed collidable faces support the entire interior lane, with at least 8.40 yards of headroom. The largest interior step is 0.270 yards. The exposed steps between outdoor ground and the entrance stairs measure 0.130 to 0.431 yards. At each entrance the extractor checks dry terrain up to the lip and verifies that a native collision face spans the exposed rise. These are static geometry checks. Live traversal on build 71091 still needs confirmation.

The pinned proofs in `tools/data/native-*-corridors-70959.json` come from the read-only Forever build 70959 snapshot. They contain native WMO placement IDs, transformed collidable floor triangles, sampled floor positions, headroom and dry root-terrain landings. The runtime does not read another addon or the client installation.

`tools/build_structure_corridors.py` checks these proofs and emits `Structures.lua`. Endpoints are bound to the registered structure; an arbitrary connector cannot borrow a floor exemption. Winding passages retain their actual world-coordinate polyline, rather than a straight shortcut through surrounding mountains. Native game north/east axes are swapped explicitly to the map library's east/north coordinates.

The four Dun Morogh gate corridors use WMO roots 110707, 110729 and 110738 with placements 73026, 87027, 171978 and 171979. Talondeep uses root 110563, placement 300408. Blackrock Mountain uses root 7952336, placement 373730. Proof hashes cover both straight and curved source files.

Preview.11 adds Ironforge root 7801267/placement 7706 and Undercity root 7675285/placement 239598. Ironforge's 274.874-yard entrance path includes three native riser witnesses (maximum 0.277778 yard), 1,166 supported samples and minimum 4.686-yard clearance. Undercity's 477.838-yard static sewer path uses 2,188 samples with minimum 4.507-yard clearance, avoids the native WMO liquid pool, and uses no elevator. Both end at native interior floors whose XY already belongs to the existing permitted city mask. Root terrain sits 315.185 yards above the Ironforge anchor and 97.067 yards above the Undercity anchor; that terrain is not used as evidence of their floor heights. Whole-city indoor geometry is not certified by these entrance paths.

Timbermaw endpoints use existing ground permission. Their components contain native Bloodvenom Post, Emerald Sanctuary and Everlook service anchors. A component ID of 1 means the largest white region in the map rectangle, which can belong to a neighbouring zone; it does not necessarily mean the playable region of the named zone. No supplemental Timbermaw ground mask was required.

Extraction follows the native WMO collision flags documented in [TrinityCore's WMO extractor](https://github.com/TrinityCore/TrinityCore/blob/master/src/tools/vmap4_extractor/wmo.cpp): retain collidable faces with flag 0x08 or flag 0x20 without flag 0x04. Placement transforms were checked against native bounds. Curved corridors check selected triangles and dense samples, dry/no-hole/no-liquid landings, headroom and bounded portal steps. Blackrock's exact-overlap route avoids the rounded face-contact gaps found during the audit. Embedded model body-column checks are supporting evidence, not swept-radius collision validation.

`GroundCorrections.lua` restores 5,048 **originally dry, permitted** raw terrain cells in two Dun Morogh outpost valleys. Root terrain cleanup had classified them as enclosed because it did not know the gate tunnels. `tools/build_ground_corrections.py` accepts only raw dry components reached by audited structure landings, rejects components touching existing permitted ground or exceeding its safety bound, and pins dimensions and the original base-mask hash. It does not erase mountain restrictions or grant walking permission to WMO roof terrain.

The four Dun Algaz tunnel placements also open three enclosed outdoor valleys in the Wetlands. Starting at the Wetlands end and walking toward Loch Modan, those valleys contain 480, 1,305 and 325 dry cells. All 2,110 cells come from the native terrain grid. Each valley connects the mouths of two successive tunnels. The terrain and liquid masks keep the intervening mountains blocked, and crossing each ridge requires its verified tunnel floor.

`WalkingRegions.lua` labels exact four-neighbor connected ground components for all 55 covered masks, including these corrections. Safe-diagonal pathfinding cannot connect different four-neighbor components. The planner uses these labels to reject impossible endpoint pairs before spending its search budget. Labels are pinned to base and supplemental mask hashes; stale labels are ignored. This is a planning optimization, not additional terrain permission.

Southfury stone bridge (Ashenvale to Azshara) uses root 113275, group 113276 and placement 294532. `tools/build_southfury_bridge.py` reproduces `tools/data/native-southfury-bridge-70959.json` from the pinned snapshot. Its 60.012-yard deck has 242 centerline samples, maximum sampled step 0.164029 yard, open-sky headroom and exact dry bank contacts in native areas 2457 (Nightsong Woods/Ashenvale) and 878 (Southfury River/Azshara). Another 5,082 native floor samples cover the central strip within 2.5 yards. The transformed mesh lies inside native placement bounds; `transformError` measures the inverse-transform roundtrip, not equality between conservative bounds and mesh extrema. Source hashes and positive bounds slack are recorded separately. The builder includes this proof in its combined digest. Width-verified bridge corridors enable partial-deck route endpoints through graph access edges, never root-terrain permission. Runtime checks reject another world instance and, when nearby readable nonzero player height is available, positions beneath the bridge. Missing native height cannot prove the player's floor and remains a live-client limitation.

The first all-bridges audit batch adds ten yaw-only native WMO corridors: nine within zones and one between the Barrens and Durotar. `tools/build_bridge_batch.py` records each sampled deck, central strip, native bounds containment and permitted dry contacts in `tools/data/native-bridge-batch-70959.json`. Together with Southfury, eleven bridges support partial-deck endpoints. The batch records native asset hashes and ownership-table/runtime terrain inputs; the audit keeps tilted, compound and unsupported-contact placements unresolved. This is a partial coverage improvement, not an all-bridges completion claim.

The expanded pass adds 65 verified placements in `tools/data/native-bridge-expanded-70959.json`: 54 WMO and 11 smaller-model paths, including 29 tilted placements and supported strips with radii from 0.5 to 2.5 yards. `tools/bridge_geometry.py` reads native M2 collision arrays, WMO groups and active doodad sets, checks native placement witnesses and performs world-vertical layered floor queries. `tools/build_bridge_expanded.py` extracts straight or bounded-search paths; `tools/verify_bridge_expanded.py` independently re-probes every centerline and lateral sample, dry bank and native liquid upper bound. One deck was withheld after the liquid check. Unclassified floor candidates are retained as audit evidence but do not become bridge permissions. The total explicitly supported bridge placements is 76; this does not certify every bridge in the game.

The placement rotation convention is documented by [TrinityCore's ModelInstance implementation](https://github.com/TrinityCore/TrinityCore/blob/master/src/common/Collision/Models/ModelInstance.cpp); native M2 collision arrays are described by its [model extractor](https://github.com/TrinityCore/TrinityCore/blob/master/src/tools/vmap4_extractor/model.cpp). These tools read the pinned client snapshot directly. Native file paths from the pinned [wow-listfile release](https://github.com/wowdev/wow-listfile/releases/tag/202611211242) supplement internal names for discovery/classification; they do not prove walkability. Proofs pin collision/placement/terrain source hashes and the resolved asset-path digest.

The subsequent Stranglethorn audit adds the northern road bridge leading toward Duskwood, bringing the total to 77 bridge placements. Model 189411 (`DuskwoodChasmBridge`), placement 44103, spans the gorge near Stranglethorn (40.7, 3.7). Its two banks meet different sides of the native deck. The 39.17-yard path uses a short approach parallel to the bridge at each end and a diagonal between them. All 167 centerline positions and 835 lateral samples have collision-floor support. The largest native floor interval gap is zero. Centerline headroom is at least 5.46 yards. At both ends, the full one-yard strip meets dry terrain with less than a 0.3-yard step. Corridor 479338901316938 connects these bank positions within map 1434.

To reproduce this placement from build 70959, run `python tools/build_bridge_expanded.py --only 0:m2:44103`, then `python tools/verify_bridge_expanded.py`, then `python tools/build_structure_corridors.py`. The proof records the native collision and ADT hashes and ten dry bank samples. Live-client traversal remains unverified.

Runtime access uses finite verified strips, without unproved round caps beyond landing planes. Remote destinations may name the deck, but new live deck starts require readable nonzero player elevation (this client also uses zero as an unavailable placeholder). Existing route guidance can continue using XY; it cannot verify a player's vertical floor without elevation. See `docs/testing-bridge-expanded.md` for live checks and `docs/bridges-and-crossings-progress.md` for unresolved cases and the deferred transform-error metadata measurement.
