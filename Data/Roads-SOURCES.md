# Road preference provenance

Generated by `tools/build_roads.py` from the read-only WoW Forever 1.60.1 build 70959 CASC snapshot. Native WDT MAID texture-ADT references, MDID texture IDs, MCLY layers and MCAL alpha maps supply the geometry. The small read-only CASC reader in `tools/roads/local_casc.py` and zone metadata are copied from our own Gatherly tooling; no runtime addon dependency is introduced.

Texture names: [wowdev/wow-listfile release 202611211242](https://github.com/wowdev/wow-listfile/releases/tag/202611211242). Texture filenames containing `road` or `cobblestone` are selected. Other layers occlude roads according to native alpha blending. Generic dirt does not imply a road. Effective mean coverage of at least 25% in a terrain cell marks it as road. Masks use the exact terrain-grid dimensions, bounds and approximately 4.17-yard resolution.

Complete/partial describes availability of texture tiles and names, not verification of every road. Town paving and docks may count as roads; dirt paths, geometry-only roads, bridges and indoor floors can be absent. Missing cells remain ordinary non-road costs and never become walkability restrictions. Terrain masks remain authoritative. These are preference hints, not collision geometry or a complete road graph.

Coverage: 55 local map masks and preview overlays, 43 with complete indexed texture coverage and 12 partial. Fifty maps contain selected road cells. Tanaris, Un'Goro Crater, Silithus, Alterac Valley and Arathi Basin contain no selected road cells and use ordinary terrain routing. Legacy four-bit and modern eight-bit/compressed alpha layers are supported.

Road walking costs 1 per yard; off-road walking costs 1.35 per yard. Smoothing preserves weighted cost as well as terrain safety. The admissible search heuristic uses physical distance, and displayed length/time remain physical estimates. The option is off by default and affects smart routing only.

Rebuild from the repository root: `python tools/build_roads.py --snapshot-root <snapshot>`. This fetches the pinned community listfile on the first run. Local output `../output/road-overlays-70959/index.html` contains all generated previews, masks, transparent overlays and an inventory with missing tiles and per-mask SHA256 hashes. Raw client map artwork is for local inspection and is not bundled or published.
